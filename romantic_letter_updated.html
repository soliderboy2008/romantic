My love,

I know I have hurt you, and I am truly sorry. I wish I could take back every word, every action, and every moment that made you feel anything less than loved. You never deserved to be hurt by the person who is supposed to make you feel safe and cherished.

I love you more than I sometimes know how to express. You are the first person who found a place in my heart like this, and the one I want to keep choosing. You are so precious to me—not because everything is always perfect, but because you are you, and there is no one who could simply take your place.

I know saying “I'm sorry” cannot magically fix what happened. I don't expect you to forget your hurt just because I wrote you a letter. I only hope you can give me the chance to show you, through my actions and not just my words, that I understand where I went wrong and that I want to do better.

You deserve patience, honesty, tenderness, and a love that makes you feel appreciated every single day. I want to listen to you better, understand you more, and be more careful with your heart. I know I am not perfect, but I don't want to use that as an excuse. I want to grow, especially in the ways that matter to us.

Even on the difficult days, my feelings for you are real. I miss your presence, your voice, your little ways of making an ordinary moment feel special. I hope you never doubt how much you mean to me. You are not just someone I love; you are someone I am grateful to have in my life.

A promise from me
I can't promise that I will never make a mistake, but I can promise to take responsibility, to keep learning, and to never treat your feelings as something small. I want my actions to make you feel the love that I keep trying to put into words.

Please forgive me when your heart is ready. Take the time you need. I love you, and I hope I can make you feel that love in a much better way from now on.

Thank you for being you, and for having a place in my heart that no one else has ever had.

With all my love,
Your loving boyfriend,
Mohaned.
